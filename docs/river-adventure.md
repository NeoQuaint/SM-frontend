# River adventure

The River world uses the supplied Maya recordings in `public/voice/river-maya/`.
The original Mountain, Lake House and Garden recordings remain separate.

`kids-river-adventure.js` owns the River clue text, recording names and SVG scenery.
Clue text remains aligned with the recording at every clue difficulty setting.
The clue button highlights the answer and repeats that recording.

| Stop | Recordings in order |
| --- | --- |
| Willow bank | Fish, Boat, Frog, Turtle, Flower |
| River bridge | Stems, Dragonfly, Rock, Bridge, Paddle |
| Waterfall | Shell, Ripple, Vest, Tadpole, Waterfall |

Willow and Pause play at the two checkpoints. Automatic travel starts one second
after the recording ends; manual continuation and exit still work. Meet plays
during the Coco reunion, before the Kids Remix finale.

Discovered props are drawn into the river scene. The bridge crossing uses an
aligned deck and walking path; the life jacket stays on Maya after discovery.
Waterfall flow, river current and animal motion respect reduced-motion settings.
No audio was uploaded for processing.
