# SmartClass Story Time — six new adventures

Six chapters per story, four short illustrated scenes per chapter. Scene narration advances only after the current passage finishes; chapter endings pause a little longer. The original v2 library remains in the repository for reference.

Artwork was generated with the built-in image tool using the existing mascot references. Sources are optimised into public/story-scenes-v3; each page specifies its exact atlas crop. Lily’s second sheet contains extra transition panels: its selected frames are mapped explicitly rather than assuming a regular twelve-frame sequence.

## Maya and the Door Beneath the Puddle

One splash. An upside-down world.

### 1. A Very Strange Splash

**Scene 1**

Rain had left shiny puddles along the path. Maya leaned over the biggest one. Her reflection waved before she did! Maya held very still. The little purple face in the water winked.

*Illustration: Maya crouches by a puddle on a bright wet garden path; her reflection winks independently.*

**Scene 2**

A tiny blue fish poked its nose through the puddle. It wore a red postbag. “Delivery downstairs!” it called. Then it dived. Maya bent closer. A little staircase shimmered beneath the water.

*Illustration: Close view of a tiny blue fish with red postbag emerging from Maya’s magical puddle.*

**Scene 3**

“Downstairs?” said Maya. She tapped the water with one toe. The puddle stretched into a soft, silver slide. Whoosh! Down she went, turning slowly like a leaf in the wind.

*Illustration: Maya slides safely down a wide silver water slide through a glowing magical tunnel.*

**Scene 4**

Plop! Maya landed on something dry. A lamp hung beside her feet. A table stood far above her head. She looked around with round eyes. “I have landed on the ceiling!”

*Illustration: Maya stands on the ceiling inside an upside-down pastel house, astonished at furniture overhead.*

### 2. Walking on the Ceiling

**Scene 5**

The post-fish swam in through a window. “You are the right way up for here,” it said. Maya took one careful step. Then another. Her feet stayed where she put them.

*Illustration: Maya tests walking along the ceiling while the blue post-fish floats nearby.*

**Scene 6**

Outside, an upside-down street curved between colourful houses. Fish swam through open windows. In a flowerpot, a little cloud rumbled. Maya tickled its edge. It watered its own pot!

*Illustration: Wide upside-down town with window-swimming fish and potted clouds; Maya touches a tiny raincloud.*

**Scene 7**

A yellow boot floated past carrying bread. “Mind your breakfast!” called a baker. Maya ducked beneath it. Up here, bread travelled by boot. She giggled. What else could this town do?

*Illustration: A floating yellow boot filled with bread sails past Maya at an upside-down bakery.*

**Scene 8**

Knock. Knock. BOOM! A sound rolled along the street. The flowerpot clouds shook. The post-fish pointed towards a staircase. “That comes from the little door at the top.”

*Illustration: Maya and post-fish turn toward a tall distant staircase as clouds tremble at a deep knock.*

### 3. The Upstairs River

**Scene 9**

A river ran up the stairs. Little leaves sailed against the steps, climbing higher and higher. Maya watched one reach the top. “That leaf knows the way,” she said.

*Illustration: Maya studies a turquoise river flowing uphill on a broad staircase, leaves floating upward.*

**Scene 10**

The post-fish brought a wide green leaf boat. Maya climbed aboard and sat in the middle. “Inside the boat,” said the fish. “And away we go!” The water lifted them gently.

*Illustration: Maya sits securely inside a wide green leaf boat beside post-fish at the staircase base.*

**Scene 11**

Up one step. Up another. Their boat bobbed past a balcony hung with drying rainbows. Maya waved to a fish folding a small red rainbow. The enormous knocking grew louder.

*Illustration: Leaf boat carries Maya up waterfall stairs past a balcony of bright drying rainbows.*

**Scene 12**

At the top, the river curled into a quiet pool. Maya stepped onto a wide landing. There was the door: small, round and blue. Behind it, someone gave a very large sniff.

*Illustration: Maya exits leaf boat onto landing facing a tiny round blue door, listening intently.*

### 4. The Enormous Knock

**Scene 13**

Maya knocked softly. “Hello in there!” A tiny voice answered, “Hello out there.” Then came another enormous sniff. Maya crouched beside the keyhole. A huge blue eye blinked back.

*Illustration: Maya crouches at a tiny door; an enormous gentle blue eye is visible through the opening.*

**Scene 14**

“I am Pip,” said the voice. “I followed a bubble through this door. Now I cannot fit back.” Maya pushed the door open a little. A round blue nose squeezed into the gap.

*Illustration: Tiny blue door opens slightly; a soft blue whale nose gently peeks through while Maya watches.*

**Scene 15**

Maya found a golden ribbon around the doorframe. She pulled one end. The little doorway stretched wider! She pulled again. Wider still. “I think this door has growing room!”

*Illustration: Maya pulls a golden ribbon, magically widening the circular blue doorway.*

**Scene 16**

Through came a flipper. Then a tail. Then all of Pip: an enormous baby whale floating in the air! Maya tipped her head right back. “You are a very big baby.”

*Illustration: Enormous friendly baby blue whale floats through expanded doorway above tiny delighted Maya.*

### 5. A Whale of a Sneeze

**Scene 17**

Pip took a deep breath. His cheeks puffed. A tickly rainbow feather fluttered on his nose. “Ah,” he said. “Ah.” Maya caught the ribbon. The post-fish held its hat.

*Illustration: Whale Pip has puffed cheeks and rainbow feather on nose; Maya braces with ribbon.*

**Scene 18**

“CHOO!” Out flew a thousand shining bubbles. The leaf boat rose. The potted clouds rose. Even the bakery rose! Everything floated slowly, as if the town were taking a big breath.

*Illustration: Whale sneezes sparkling bubbles; pastel houses, leaf boat and potted clouds float gently upward.*

**Scene 19**

Maya landed in a floating bread boot. She reached out and caught the post-fish’s bag. “Got you!” Together they drifted beside Pip. Below them, the whole town bobbed like bath toys.

*Illustration: Maya rides a floating yellow bread boot holding the post-fish bag beside airborne whale and town.*

**Scene 20**

“Blow gently this time,” Maya said. Pip made a small, slow puff. The bubbles drifted down. Houses settled. Pots landed. The bread boot slid neatly back outside the bakery.

*Illustration: Whale blows gently as bubble-carried houses and flowerpots settle safely back into place.*

### 6. Home Through the Rain

**Scene 21**

One bubble stayed behind. Inside it was Maya’s garden, shining in the afternoon sun. “Your way home,” said the post-fish. Pip lowered a flipper beside it. Maya climbed up.

*Illustration: Pip holds a flipper beside a large bubble showing Maya’s sunny home garden.*

**Scene 22**

Maya waved from Pip’s flipper. “Goodbye, upstairs river! Goodbye, ceiling street!” She stepped through the garden bubble. It felt cool and soft, like walking through a curtain of rain.

*Illustration: Maya steps from whale flipper through a glowing garden bubble, waving to the post-fish.*

**Scene 23**

Splash! Maya stood beside the same puddle. Her feet were on the ground. The clouds were in the sky. Everything looked ordinary. Then a tiny yellow bread boot floated past her toe.

*Illustration: Maya returns to original garden puddle; tiny yellow bread boot floats on its surface.*

**Scene 24**

Maya crouched and waved. Far below, a blue tail waved back. “Bless you, Pip,” she whispered. From beneath the puddle came one very small, very polite sneeze. Plip!

*Illustration: Maya smiles gently at blue whale tail reflected deep in puddle; one tiny sparkling bubble rises.*

## Lily and the Runaway Full Stop

A tiny dot. A train that cannot stop.

### 1. The Dot That Ran

**Scene 1**

Lily opened a big picture book on the carpet. On the first page, a red train waited beside a station. Under the picture was a little black dot. It twitched.

*Illustration: Lily blue fluffy cloud mascot opens a large picture book showing a red train and black dot.*

**Scene 2**

Two tiny feet popped out of the dot. It stretched, yawned and hopped away from the sentence. “Wait!” said Lily. “You belong at the end!” The dot bounced into the picture.

*Illustration: Small black full stop with two tiny feet jumps off book lettering; Lily looks surprised.*

**Scene 3**

The train gave a whistle. Its wheels began turning. The dot ran beside the tracks. Lily reached into the page. Her hand went right through! Warm station air tickled her fingers.

*Illustration: Lily reaches a hand into magical book while red train begins moving inside the illustration.*

**Scene 4**

Lily stepped onto the platform inside the book. Behind her, the open page stood like a giant doorway. “Ticket?” asked a duck in a blue cap. Lily pointed at the runaway dot.

*Illustration: Lily stands inside a bright illustrated railway station with duck conductor and huge page doorway behind.*

### 2. All Aboard the Page

**Scene 5**

“Our full stop!” quacked the duck. “Without it, this story keeps going.” Lily hopped into the first carriage. The dot was already on the roof, dancing beside the chimney.

*Illustration: Lily boards first carriage while black dot dances on roof of small red engine.*

**Scene 6**

The train passed a little pond. Three ducks waddled aboard. One, two, three. Click! A new carriage joined the back. “Lovely,” said the conductor. “We have room for ducks.”

*Illustration: Three ducks board a new yellow carriage beside a pond while Lily counts them.*

**Scene 7**

At the next crossing, a bathtub rolled towards the track on four little wheels. Click! Another carriage caught it. Water splashed. A rubber duck squeaked. Lily blinked twice.

*Illustration: Wheeled bathtub with rubber duck rolls onto an added carriage; Lily looks amazed.*

**Scene 8**

“Does this train collect everything?” Lily asked. The conductor opened his beak to answer. A birthday cake floated past the window. Behind it came balloons, chairs and a whole birthday party.

*Illustration: A floating birthday cake and balloons approach Lily’s train, followed by party furniture.*

### 3. A Very Long Train

**Scene 9**

Click, click, click! The party landed in three new carriages. The guests kept singing. The cake kept wobbling. Lily held one balloon string so it would not tickle the driver.

*Illustration: Birthday party fills three train carriages; Lily catches a balloon while cake wobbles safely.*

**Scene 10**

The train stretched around a hill. Lily could see its front and its back at the same time. “That is a very long story,” she said. The dot skipped from roof to roof.

*Illustration: Long colourful train curves around a rounded hill; Lily sees dot hopping across carriage roofs.*

**Scene 11**

Lily followed inside the carriages. Through the ducks. Around the bathtub. Under the party bunting. Each door led to something new. She was getting closer to the tiny tapping feet.

*Illustration: Lily carefully travels through connected carriage interiors past ducks, bathtub and birthday bunting.*

**Scene 12**

At the last carriage, the dot bounced onto an open page hanging above the track. Lily stopped beneath it. On that page was a drawing of a bridge. Half the bridge was missing.

*Illustration: Lily notices black dot on hanging book page above a track leading toward an unfinished bridge.*

### 4. The Edge of the Story

**Scene 13**

The train rounded the bend. Ahead, the paper track ended at a wide white space. Beyond it was the edge of the book. The driver whistled. “We need our stopping dot!”

*Illustration: Red train approaches blank white paper gap at book edge; Lily and driver look alert, not terrified.*

**Scene 14**

Lily found a blue crayon in the party carriage. She knelt at the open window and drew a broad curve. A new track appeared beneath her line. The train turned with it.

*Illustration: Lily draws a broad blue crayon curve that becomes a safe new railway track.*

**Scene 15**

Round they went, back across the page. The ducks cheered. The bathtub sloshed. Lily had made a loop! It would give her time, but the train was still going.

*Illustration: Train travels a new looping blue track around the page; ducks cheer and Lily concentrates.*

**Scene 16**

Lily watched the dot. Bounce, bounce, pause. Bounce, bounce, pause. It always landed on the same little roof. “I know your pattern,” she whispered. She fetched the duck’s empty ticket box.

*Illustration: Lily watches rhythmic black-dot hops and holds a small open ticket box.*

### 5. Catch That Dot

**Scene 17**

She set the box on the roof and waited inside the carriage. Bounce. Bounce. Plop! The dot landed in the box. Lily closed the lid gently. Tiny feet tapped underneath.

*Illustration: Black dot lands in ticket box on low carriage roof; Lily gently closes lid.*

**Scene 18**

“You can run again later,” Lily told it. “First, we need an ending.” She carried the box to the front. The driver pointed at the sentence beside the track. There was a gap.

*Illustration: Lily carries ticket box to engine; duck driver points toward unfinished sentence with clear ending gap.*

**Scene 19**

Lily opened the box beside the gap. The dot peeked out. It took one little step, then another. At the end of the sentence it sat down, tucked in its feet, and became still.

*Illustration: Lily places black full stop at sentence ending; tiny feet fold away.*

**Scene 20**

The train stopped. The cake stopped wobbling. The bathwater stopped splashing. Even the balloons stood still. For one quiet moment, the whole picture held its breath. “We did it,” whispered Lily.

*Illustration: Entire train and party settle peacefully; Lily exhales, balloons and cake still.*

### 6. One More Little Adventure

**Scene 21**

The driver opened the doors. Ducks waddled to the pond. Guests carried their cake home. The bathtub rolled away with a small squeak. Lily watched every passenger reach the right place.

*Illustration: At station, ducks leave for pond, guests carry cake and wheeled bathtub rolls away.*

**Scene 22**

The full stop sat neatly at the end of the story. Lily gave it a tiny bow. It gave her a tiny wink. Then she stepped through the open page onto her own carpet.

*Illustration: Lily bows to black dot then steps from giant open book doorway onto home carpet.*

**Scene 23**

She closed the book carefully. The room was quiet. From between the pages came a soft quack. Lily opened the cover just a crack. The conductor was looking up at her.

*Illustration: Lily on carpet opens book a crack; small duck conductor peeks from between pages.*

**Scene 24**

“Again?” asked the duck. Lily laughed. “Tomorrow.” She put a bookmark beside the station. This time, the full stop stayed exactly where it belonged. But its little toes were still wiggling.

*Illustration: Peaceful book on carpet with bookmark at station and black dot showing tiny playful toes; Lily laughs.*

## Suzy and the Circus of Giant Shadows

A spoon becomes a slide. A shadow steals the show.

### 1. The Extra Arms

**Scene 1**

The circus had closed for the evening. Suzy carried a little lantern past the striped tent. Her shadow stretched across the canvas. She waved one arm. Her shadow waved three.

*Illustration: Orange octopus Suzy holds lantern outside striped circus tent at dusk; shadow has extra waving arms.*

**Scene 2**

Suzy lowered every arm. Her shadow kept waving. “Excuse me,” she said. “Those are not my arms.” The shadow gave an enormous bow. Then it lifted the edge of the tent.

*Illustration: Suzy stares curiously as her independent many-armed shadow bows and lifts tent curtain.*

**Scene 3**

A strip of golden light shone underneath. Suzy crouched and peeked. Beyond the curtain was another circus, much bigger than the first. A tiny mouse stood beside a giant drum.

*Illustration: Suzy peers under raised circus curtain into golden shadow circus; tiny mouse beside enormous drum silhouette.*

**Scene 4**

“Welcome to rehearsal!” squeaked the mouse. Suzy slipped inside. Her shadow settled behind her again. In the middle of the ring stood a lamp, a spoon and a small round button.

*Illustration: Suzy enters circular circus ring with mouse, central lamp, ordinary spoon and button on table.*

### 2. Behind the Curtain

**Scene 5**

The mouse lifted the spoon in front of the lamp. Its shadow climbed up the tent. Higher and higher! The tiny spoon had become an enormous slide. Suzy’s eyes grew wide.

*Illustration: Mouse holds small spoon before lamp creating gigantic spoon-shaped slide shadow across tent.*

**Scene 6**

“Can we slide on it?” Suzy asked. The mouse nodded. Suzy touched the dark shape. It felt smooth and springy. She climbed three soft steps and slid down with a delighted whoop.

*Illustration: Suzy slides down solid magical spoon shadow, tentacles spread playfully, mouse watches.*

**Scene 7**

Next came the button. Near the lamp, its shadow grew huge. Farther away, it became small. Suzy moved it slowly back and forth. “Big. Small. Big again!”

*Illustration: Suzy experiments moving button near and far from lamp, changing round shadow size.*

**Scene 8**

She held the button high. A round moon appeared above the ring. The mouse put on a tiny silver hat. “Now,” he said, “we need some music.” Suzy wiggled her arms.

*Illustration: Button shadow becomes giant round moon above ring; mouse in silver hat looks toward Suzy.*

### 3. A Very Small Rehearsal

**Scene 9**

Suzy tapped the floor with two arms. Tap, tap. She clapped two more. Clap, clap. The remaining arms swayed. Behind her, the shadow stretched into a whole marching band.

*Illustration: Suzy drums and claps tentacles; huge many-armed shadow band forms behind her.*

**Scene 10**

The spoon slide bounced to the beat. The button moon bobbed overhead. The mouse practised a very important bow and fell into his own hat. Suzy stopped playing to laugh.

*Illustration: Mouse tumbles harmlessly into oversized silver hat beside bouncing spoon shadow and moon.*

**Scene 11**

At the door, a row of little moths gathered. Then beetles. Then crickets carrying tiny cushions. “Our audience!” squeaked the mouse. Suzy pulled the curtain wide with four waving arms.

*Illustration: Friendly moths, beetles and crickets arrive with tiny cushions as Suzy opens curtain.*

**Scene 12**

The ring filled with warm lantern light. Suzy stood tall in the middle. Her shadow bowed to the audience. This time, Suzy bowed too. The show was ready to begin.

*Illustration: Wide circus ring with insect audience, Suzy and matching shadow bowing under warm golden lights.*

### 4. The Biggest Little Show

**Scene 13**

First, three crickets slid down the giant spoon. One landed softly. Two landed softly. The third spun all the way down and landed backwards. The audience clicked and clapped.

*Illustration: Three cricket performers descend giant spoon slide, one safely lands backwards before amused audience.*

**Scene 14**

Then the button moon rolled across the ceiling. The mouse walked beneath it, pretending to hold it up. Suzy moved the button carefully. The moon followed every little turn of her arm.

*Illustration: Mouse pretends to carry giant moon shadow while Suzy carefully positions real button near lamp.*

**Scene 15**

For the grand finale, Suzy lifted all her arms. Eight tall shadow ribbons curled across the tent. They became arches, wheels and a giant flower. The audience gazed up in wonder.

*Illustration: Suzy’s tentacle shadows transform into magnificent arches, wheels and giant flower across circus tent.*

**Scene 16**

A thin stripe of daylight slipped beneath the door. One shadow ribbon shrank. Then another. Outside, the sun was rising. Suzy looked from the doorway to her tiny, shrinking band.

*Illustration: First dawn light enters tent and shadow ribbons shrink; Suzy notices with thoughtful surprise.*

### 5. Here Comes the Sun

**Scene 17**

The mouse hurried towards the lamp. “Our show only fits in the dark!” he said. Suzy shaded it with one arm. The shadows grew a little. She shaded it with two. Bigger!

*Illustration: Suzy shades lamp with tentacles, restoring some shadow size as mouse points toward dawn.*

**Scene 18**

But daylight was spreading across the ring. Suzy looked at her little lantern. Its sides were made of pale paper. She opened the door and held the button inside. A moon appeared.

*Illustration: Suzy discovers button creates miniature moon shadow inside her translucent paper lantern.*

**Scene 19**

“A smaller circus!” she called. The mouse carried the spoon. The crickets carried their cushions. Suzy held the lantern steady while the performers stepped into the soft golden glow.

*Illustration: Mouse and crickets carry tiny circus props toward open lantern held steadily by Suzy.*

**Scene 20**

Inside the lantern, the spoon became a slide again. The button became a moon. Suzy tapped a tiny rhythm on the handle. Eight little shadow ribbons danced across the paper sides.

*Illustration: Close-up glowing paper lantern containing miniature spoon slide, moon and dancing shadow ribbons.*

### 6. A Circus to Carry Home

**Scene 21**

The big tent grew bright and ordinary. The shadow circus had packed itself into one little light. Suzy lifted it gently. From inside came a tiny drumroll and a very proud squeak.

*Illustration: Suzy lifts magical lantern in now sunlit ordinary circus tent; tiny shadow show visible inside.*

**Scene 22**

She carried it home along the morning path. The sun made a short shadow beside her. Suzy waved. Her shadow waved back at exactly the same time. “Much better,” she said.

*Illustration: Suzy walks sunny path holding lantern, her ordinary shadow now mirrors her wave correctly.*

**Scene 23**

That evening, Suzy hung the lantern beside her bed. On its paper walls, the moon rolled into place. A tiny curtain opened. The mouse stood ready in his silver hat.

*Illustration: Cosy bedroom at evening, lantern beside Suzy’s bed reveals tiny mouse and moon shadow.*

**Scene 24**

Suzy tucked her arms beneath her blanket. “Just one little show,” she whispered. Inside the lantern, a cricket slid backwards down a spoon. Suzy giggled all the way into a yawn.

*Illustration: Sleepy Suzy tucked in bed giggles at miniature backwards cricket slide glowing inside bedside lantern.*

## Coco and the Twenty-Second Rocket

Small flights. An enormous moon-sized surprise.

### 1. Somewhere Nearby

**Scene 1**

Coco found a round red rocket beside the shed. It had a blue seat, a golden button and a very small fuel tank. A picture on its door showed twenty little dots.

*Illustration: Yellow star Coco discovers small round red rocket with blue seat and golden button by garden shed.*

**Scene 2**

A tinny voice spoke from the rocket. “Twenty seconds of flying. Please choose somewhere nearby.” Coco looked up. The moon was pale and round in the afternoon sky. “There,” said Coco.

*Illustration: Coco points excitedly at pale daytime moon beside rocket with twenty-dot gauge.*

**Scene 3**

“That is not nearby,” said the rocket. Coco climbed into the seat and fastened the soft blue belt. “Then we shall go a little at a time.” The golden button began to glow.

*Illustration: Coco buckles blue safety belt inside red rocket cockpit and studies glowing golden button.*

**Scene 4**

“Three, two, one!” Coco pressed the button. Up went the rocket in a puff of silver bubbles. The shed became small. The garden became smaller. A cloud floated into view.

*Illustration: Small red rocket launches on silver bubble trail above colourful garden with Coco visible inside.*

### 2. A Cloud for a Landing

**Scene 5**

The rocket gave a polite ding. “First landing.” It settled on a broad white cloud. Coco stepped out. The cloud felt like a firm, springy pillow. A little weather shed stood nearby.

*Illustration: Red rocket lands securely on broad cloud platform beside tiny weather shed; Coco steps out.*

**Scene 6**

Inside the shed, a cloud keeper sorted raindrops into jars. Coco pointed up at the moon. “I need another little flight.” The keeper handed over a jar of sparkling wind.

*Illustration: Coco meets friendly tiny cloud keeper among rain jars and receives a jar of sparkling wind.*

**Scene 7**

“Pour slowly,” said the keeper. Coco tipped the wind into the tank. The twenty little dots lit up again. The rocket wiggled with a tickle. “That should do nicely.”

*Illustration: Coco pours sparkling wind from jar into rocket tank, twenty gauge dots glow.*

**Scene 8**

Coco waved goodbye and climbed aboard. Another countdown. Another short flight. Above the cloud, an enormous clear bubble drifted past. The rocket slipped gently through its shining side.

*Illustration: Rocket leaves cloud and passes gently through side of enormous transparent floating bubble.*

### 3. Inside a Bubble

**Scene 9**

Inside the bubble, everything floated. Coco’s map floated. A loose cushion floated. Coco stayed snug in the seat and watched the cushion turn upside down. “A very bouncy room!”

*Illustration: Inside giant bubble, belted Coco watches map and cushion float around red rocket cockpit.*

**Scene 10**

A silver fish swam through the air beside the window. On its tail was a ribbon of fizz. “Going up?” it asked. Coco nodded. The fish made a circle of tiny bubbles.

*Illustration: Silver flying fish with sparkling ribbon tail talks to Coco through rocket window inside bubble.*

**Scene 11**

Coco caught the tiny bubbles in the empty jar. One bubble popped with a cheerful ping. The others fizzed into the fuel tank. Twenty dots glowed. “Thank you, floating fish!”

*Illustration: Coco catches tiny fizz bubbles in jar beside rocket; silver fish circles cheerfully.*

**Scene 12**

The rocket slipped out of the big bubble and climbed again. Ahead was a striped hill with a feather on top. Coco landed beside the feather. The whole hill gave a soft snore.

*Illustration: Rocket lands on enormous striped hat brim with giant feather, misty sky all around.*

### 4. The Sleeping Hat

**Scene 13**

It was a hat. Under the hat was a sleeping cloud giant! Coco tiptoed around the rocket. The giant’s nose gave a long, gentle whistle. The feather lifted in the breeze.

*Illustration: Wide reveal of kind sleeping cloud giant wearing striped hat, tiny Coco and rocket on broad brim.*

**Scene 14**

Coco held up the empty jar. A little puff from the giant’s nose swirled inside. The jar hummed softly. Coco put one finger to their lips. “Quiet fuel,” they whispered.

*Illustration: Coco quietly catches a gentle visible puff of giant’s snore in glass jar on hat brim.*

**Scene 15**

The giant stirred. Coco froze beside the feather. A huge hand reached up, straightened the hat, and settled again. The rocket rocked once. Then everything was still.

*Illustration: Sleeping giant gently straightens hat while Coco braces safely beside rocket and feather.*

**Scene 16**

Coco poured in the humming puff. This time the countdown was a whisper. “Three, two, one.” The rocket rose softly. Below, the giant smiled in his sleep and rolled over.

*Illustration: Red rocket lifts softly from giant’s hat on faint silver trail, Coco waves quietly.*

### 5. The Last Little Flight

**Scene 17**

The moon filled the window. Its round hollows looked like bowls. The rocket lowered its little feet. It landed inside a wide, shallow crater with the softest possible bump.

*Illustration: Rocket lands in broad shallow silver moon crater under starry violet sky.*

**Scene 18**

Coco climbed down and took one careful step. Bong! A warm note rang through the crater. Coco looked at their foot. Then at the ground. “Did I do that?”

*Illustration: Coco touches moon crater floor with one foot as golden musical ripple spreads, surprised face.*

**Scene 19**

A small hop made a high ping. A bigger hop made a deep bong. Coco tried the next crater. Ting! Each moon bowl had its own sound. Coco began to bounce in a pattern.

*Illustration: Coco hops among adjacent shallow moon bowls with distinct glowing musical ripples.*

**Scene 20**

Ping, ping, bong. Ping, ping, bong. The craters shone with every note. Far below, the sleeping giant opened one eye. In the garden, Maya lifted her head to listen.

*Illustration: Moon craters glow in rhythmic pattern with Coco; distant Earth garden and listening giant below.*

### 6. The Moon Goes Bong

**Scene 21**

Coco played a little moon tune. The bubble fish circled in time. The cloud keeper tapped a rain jar. Below them, the giant’s enormous finger tapped gently on his hat.

*Illustration: Coco performs moon-bowl concert while bubble fish, cloud keeper and giant listen across sky.*

**Scene 22**

The rocket gave a quiet ding. Its tank now held a jarful of moon music. “Enough for the way home,” it said. Coco gave the moon one last gentle tap. Ting.

*Illustration: Coco beside rocket with glowing musical fuel tank gives moon surface one final gentle tap.*

**Scene 23**

Down they glided, past the hat, the bubble and the cloud. Each friend waved. At the garden, the rocket settled beside the shed. Coco’s friends hurried over to see.

*Illustration: Rocket descends past familiar hat, bubble and cloud toward garden where mascot friends wait.*

**Scene 24**

“Was it nearby?” asked Maya. Coco looked up at the moon and grinned. “Not at first.” From inside the rocket came a tiny ping, ping, bong. The moon had sent a tune home.

*Illustration: Coco and Maya beside landed red rocket in twilight garden, moon overhead and tiny musical glow.*

## Juppy and the Footprints That Went Up

Follow the clues. Something enormous is about to hatch.

### 1. Mud on the Leaves

**Scene 1**

Juppy found three muddy footprints beside the garden gate. They were much bigger than his own. He put one green foot beside a print. Three long toes. “Not mine,” he said.

*Illustration: Green frog Juppy compares own foot with giant three-toed muddy footprints beside garden gate.*

**Scene 2**

He fetched a magnifying glass and a small blue bag. The tracks crossed the path, passed the flowers and stopped at a tree. Juppy looked behind the trunk. Nothing. Then he looked up.

*Illustration: Juppy with magnifying glass and blue satchel follows tracks to tree and looks upward.*

**Scene 3**

Muddy footprints climbed straight up the bark. One leaf held a yellow feather. Another held a tiny striped sock. Juppy put the sock in his bag. “A bird wearing socks?”

*Illustration: Juppy discovers yellow feather and tiny striped sock on branches beside upward muddy tracks.*

**Scene 4**

A broad branch curled down like a staircase. Juppy climbed carefully, holding the rail of twisting leaves. Higher up, something white and shiny hung from a twig. It looked like a tooth.

*Illustration: Juppy climbs safe broad leafy stair-branch toward small white tooth-shaped object on twig.*

### 2. A Trail in the Tree

**Scene 5**

Juppy held the shiny thing to the light. It was smooth and hollow. “Too big for a snail,” he said. A rustle came from above. A pair of yellow feathers drifted down.

*Illustration: Juppy inspects hollow white tooth-like clue under leaves as yellow feathers drift downward.*

**Scene 6**

At the treetop, a cloud rested against a wide branch. The muddy tracks continued across it. Juppy touched the cloud with his magnifying glass. It was firm as a cushion.

*Illustration: Juppy at broad treetop branch discovers solid cloud walkway marked by muddy footprints.*

**Scene 7**

He took one careful step. Then another. The cloud held him. Ahead, a whole path of clouds curved across the sky. Something far away went cluck, creak, cluck, creak.

*Illustration: Juppy walks across broad safe cloud path with faraway house silhouette ahead.*

**Scene 8**

Juppy stopped to listen. The cluck came first. The creak came next. “Feet and something wooden,” he whispered. He followed the sound around a tall cloud shaped like a cauliflower.

*Illustration: Juppy listens beside huge cauliflower-shaped cloud, following distant clucking and creaking.*

### 3. Footprints in the Clouds

**Scene 9**

There it was: a little house walking on two enormous chicken feet! Curtains flapped in its windows. A kettle whistled from the chimney. Every step left a muddy print on the cloud.

*Illustration: Reveal cosy small wooden house walking on huge yellow chicken feet across clouds, kettle chimney.*

**Scene 10**

“Excuse me!” called Juppy. The house stopped. A tiny snail leaned out of the window. It wore one striped sock on its tail. “Have you seen my other sock?” it asked.

*Illustration: Tiny snail wearing one striped sock leans from walking house window to greet Juppy.*

**Scene 11**

Juppy opened his bag. The snail cheered. “That is it!” Juppy showed the yellow feather too. The chicken feet shuffled proudly. At last, two clues had found their answers.

*Illustration: Juppy returns tiny striped sock and shows feather; snail cheers from low house window.*

**Scene 12**

“And this?” Juppy held up the white tooth. The snail blinked. “That is not a tooth. It is a piece of my doorstep.” A little white chip was missing beneath the door.

*Illustration: Juppy matches white tooth-shaped shard to chipped white doorstep of walking house.*

### 4. The Walking House

**Scene 13**

The house lowered itself onto the cloud. A little ladder unfolded. “Come for tea,” said the snail. Juppy climbed aboard, ducked through the door, and hung his blue bag on a peg.

*Illustration: House crouches on cloud with ladder unfolded; Juppy boards through cosy front door.*

**Scene 14**

Inside were a tiny kitchen and a very large rocking chair. “I wanted to travel without leaving home,” said the snail. “So I planted my house in a nest.” Juppy blinked.

*Illustration: Juppy and tiny snail in whimsical house kitchen with large rocking chair and round walls.*

**Scene 15**

Tap. Tap. Tap. The floor trembled gently. Juppy set down his cup. A crack ran across the wall, but behind it was something soft and yellow. The snail leaned closer.

*Illustration: Juppy and snail inspect gentle crack in round house wall revealing soft yellow down.*

**Scene 16**

“This house is an egg,” said Juppy. Another tap came from underneath. The kettle wobbled. The snail opened a cupboard. Inside was a folded picnic blanket and one tiny safety whistle.

*Illustration: Juppy realises house is huge egg as snail opens cupboard containing picnic blanket and whistle.*

### 5. A House With a Heartbeat

**Scene 17**

“Time for a picnic outside,” said the snail. Together they carried the blanket, cups and biscuits down the ladder. They spread everything on the wide cloud and sat a little way back.

*Illustration: Juppy and snail move tea things onto broad cloud picnic blanket safely away from egg-house.*

**Scene 18**

The cracks joined in a zigzag. The roof lifted like a hat. A round yellow head peeked out. Two fluffy wings unfolded. The chicken feet had belonged to a giant sky chick all along!

*Illustration: Giant fluffy yellow sky chick hatches from round house shell, roof lifting gently; friends watch.*

**Scene 19**

The chick shook its feathers. The empty house shell opened into two neat halves. One became a little shelter. The other became a bowl. Juppy caught the kettle before it rolled away.

*Illustration: Newly hatched sky chick shakes feathers; egg-house halves form shelter and bowl beside picnic.*

**Scene 20**

The chick bent its enormous head to sniff a biscuit. “One for you,” said Juppy. The biscuit vanished in one tiny peck. The snail pushed over the whole plate. “Perhaps two.”

### 6. The First Flying Picnic

**Scene 21**

The chick lowered one soft wing beside the blanket. Juppy and the snail climbed into a wide feather nest on its back. The picnic basket fitted between them. “Ready?” whispered Juppy.

*Illustration: Juppy and snail settle securely in feather nest on giant chick’s back with picnic basket.*

**Scene 22**

Up rose the chick. Slowly at first, then smoothly across the clouds. Below, the treetop path became a green ribbon. Juppy watched the garden come into view and pointed towards home.

*Illustration: Giant yellow sky chick flies gently across clouds carrying Juppy and snail toward garden.*

**Scene 23**

The chick landed beside the gate. Maya looked at the giant feet. Then at Juppy. Then at the snail. “You followed the footprints?” she asked. Juppy held up his magnifying glass.

**Scene 24**

“All the way up,” he said. They spread the blanket beneath a tree. Above it, a giant yellow wing made a lovely patch of shade. The snail finally put on its other sock.

## The Friends Who Unzipped the Night

Behind the sky, tomorrow is waiting.

### 1. A Golden Zip

**Scene 1**

The friends were finishing a picnic as the sky turned purple. Coco noticed a golden line above the hill. It had little teeth and a round handle. “That looks like a zip,” said Lily.

*Illustration: Five SmartClass mascots at twilight picnic spot a huge golden zipper across violet sky.*

**Scene 2**

Maya reached from the top of the little hill. The handle was warm. She gave it a small pull. Zzzzip. A narrow opening appeared in the night, glowing softly at the edges.

*Illustration: Maya gently pulls reachable golden sky zipper from hilltop while friends watch with wonder.*

**Scene 3**

Behind the opening stood a staircase made of folded clouds. Warm light shone at the top. “We can look together,” said Maya. The friends climbed slowly, with Juppy bringing the picnic basket.

*Illustration: Five friends climb broad folded-cloud staircase through glowing opening in evening sky.*

**Scene 4**

At the top was an enormous workshop. Stars waited in trays. Clouds hung from a washing line. Across the floor lay a rolled-up golden carpet. A tiny moth hurried towards them.

*Illustration: Reveal magical night workshop with star trays, drying clouds, rolled gold sunrise and spectacled moth.*

### 2. Behind the Stars

**Scene 5**

The moth wore spectacles much bigger than its face. “Just in time,” it squeaked. “Tomorrow is nearly ready.” Suzy pointed at the golden carpet. “Is that the sunrise?” The moth nodded proudly.

*Illustration: Tiny moth with oversized spectacles shows Suzy rolled golden sunrise carpet in workshop.*

**Scene 6**

Lily peered into a tray of dull little stars. Coco touched a soft cloth beside it. “These need polishing,” said the moth. Juppy found a basket of clouds, still damp with yesterday’s rain.

*Illustration: Lily and Coco inspect star polishing tray while Juppy discovers basket of damp clouds.*

**Scene 7**

Maya unrolled one corner of the sunrise. Pink light spilled across her paws. Beneath it was orange, then gold. “We must keep the colours in order,” said the moth. Maya rolled it back.

*Illustration: Maya carefully opens corner of layered pink orange gold sunrise carpet lighting her paws.*

**Scene 8**

In the far corner sat the sun, round and warm inside a little wooden cradle. Suzy gave it the gentlest tap. It rocked. She tapped once more. This time, it rolled over the edge.

*Illustration: Suzy accidentally tips small warm glowing sun out of wooden cradle, surprised expression.*

### 3. Making Tomorrow

**Scene 9**

The sun rolled across the floor. Slowly, then faster. “Round things roll!” called Coco. It bumped a cloud cushion, turned a corner and disappeared towards the stairs. The friends hurried after it.

*Illustration: Small glowing sun rolls across workshop past cloud cushion as five mascots follow.*

**Scene 10**

Maya reached the stairs first. The sun bounced down one step. Then two. Each bounce made a little dawn flash outside. Somewhere below, a sleepy rooster opened one eye.

*Illustration: Maya watches sun bounce down cloud steps, little sunrise flashes illuminating sleepy rooster far below.*

**Scene 11**

Juppy spread the picnic blanket across a wide landing. Lily held one corner. Maya held another. The sun rolled into the middle and settled with a soft, warm wobble.

*Illustration: Maya Lily Juppy catch warm sun gently in picnic blanket across broad staircase landing.*

**Scene 12**

Suzy came down carrying the empty cradle. “I will hold it still,” she said. Together they tipped the sun back inside. Coco counted the corners of the blanket as they folded it away.

*Illustration: Suzy holds cradle steady as friends carefully return sun, Coco folds picnic blanket.*

### 4. Catch the Sun

**Scene 13**

Back in the workshop, the moth checked its tiny clock. There was still time. “One job each,” said Maya. Coco chose the stars. Lily chose the cloud line. Juppy carried the damp basket.

*Illustration: Five friends choose workshop jobs with spectacled moth and tiny clock in background.*

**Scene 14**

Coco polished the stars in a simple pattern. Bright, brighter, brightest! A tiny star squeaked when Coco rubbed its point. “Ticklish,” said Coco. The moth placed each finished star in a tray.

*Illustration: Coco polishes stars with cloth, small star reacting playfully while moth arranges tray.*

**Scene 15**

Lily and Juppy hung the last damp clouds on the line. Big clouds on big pegs. Small clouds on small pegs. A little breeze passed through them, shaking out soft silver drops.

*Illustration: Lily and Juppy hang differently sized clouds on matching clothes pegs as silver drops fall.*

**Scene 16**

Suzy held the sun’s cradle with four arms. With the others, she turned its little handles. Up it rose on a golden lift. “Slowly this time,” she said. The sun stayed snug and still.

*Illustration: Suzy carefully operates golden lift carrying sun securely in cradle, focused expression.*

### 5. A Sky Full of Almost

**Scene 17**

Maya began unrolling the sunrise across a long frame. Pink first. Then orange. Then gold. The colours spread without mixing into a muddle. Outside, the very bottom of the sky began to glow.

*Illustration: Maya unrolls pink orange gold sunrise across long frame; horizon outside begins glowing.*

**Scene 18**

The moth opened a small hatch. Juppy passed the clouds through, one at a time. Lily guided them into the sky. A long thin cloud stretched itself into a sleepy smile.

*Illustration: Juppy passes fluffy clouds through hatch while Lily arranges them in glowing dawn sky.*

**Scene 19**

Coco carried the polished star tray to another hatch. The moth shook its head gently. “Those are for tonight.” Coco laughed and put them safely on the shelf. Tomorrow had a bedtime too.

*Illustration: Coco smilingly puts polished stars on night shelf as moth explains beside dawn hatch.*

**Scene 20**

The sun reached the top of its lift. Suzy stopped turning. Maya checked the sunrise. Lily checked the clouds. Juppy checked the clear path outside. The moth lifted one tiny wing. “Ready.”

*Illustration: All five mascots check completed sunrise machinery; sun at top of lift and moth signals readiness.*

### 6. Our Very Own Morning

**Scene 21**

The workshop opened like a pair of curtains. Out slipped the sun. Gold spread across the hills. Pink touched the clouds. The friends stood very still as their morning filled the world.

*Illustration: Workshop opens to spectacular sunrise over rolling hills; five friends watch in awe from threshold.*

**Scene 22**

Down below, windows glowed. Birds stretched their wings. The rooster stood tall and crowed at last. The friends waved from the cloud stairs, though nobody on the ground could see them.

*Illustration: Sunlit homes, birds and crowing rooster below; five mascots wave from broad cloud staircase.*

**Scene 23**

They climbed back to the picnic hill. Maya pulled the golden zip closed. The sky looked ordinary again, except for one small gold stitch. Suzy checked the basket. There was one biscuit left.

*Illustration: Friends return to picnic hill as Maya closes sky zipper leaving tiny gold stitch; Suzy checks basket.*

**Scene 24**

They shared the biscuit and watched the clouds drift past. One long cloud curved into a familiar smile. Coco pointed. “That is ours.” And for a while, nobody needed to say anything more.

*Illustration: Five friends quietly share biscuit on morning picnic hill beneath their smiling cloud, peaceful satisfied faces.*
