# Word World artwork

Asset: `public/lily-word-world.webp` (optimised WebP).

Created with the built-in image generation tool, using `public/lily-full-body.png` as the character reference.

Prompt: Create a portrait 2:3 full-bleed 3D preschool welcome illustration for SmartClass Lily's Word World. Preserve Lily's blue cloud-shaped full-body identity, short legs, arms and kind dark eyes. Lily holds an open picture book inside a warm children's bookshop; an orange kitten peeks over the book and C A T blocks sit beside her. Curved coral and honey-yellow bookshelves, turquoise walls, sunny rounded window, peach wooden floor and violet accents. Bright harmonious colours, soft matte sculptural texture and gentle ambient light. Keep the upper wall and lower floor calm for HTML title and button overlays. No UI, frame, logo or text other than the C A T blocks.

The welcome uses the complete image with `object-fit: contain`; the smaller category card uses a deliberate crop.

## Narration

Existing bundled word clips are reused where available. Other lines use browser speech. The blending stage currently provides a slow whole-word model, not independently recorded phonemes. Dedicated Lily recordings for individual sounds should replace that model before presenting it as a full phonics lesson. Sentence highlighting follows speech boundary events when supported by the browser.
