# Lily's lake house

The 18 supplied Lake MP3s live in `public/voice/lake-maya/`. Clue wording and
audio mappings are in `kids-lake-adventure.js`; repeating a clue uses the same
recording. Other Find adventures keep their existing audio.

The existing porch, cosy room and picnic setting are retained. Selected items
appear in context: the key opens the front door, curtains close, the lamp glows,
the cushion rests on the seat, and dishes sit on tables. The blanket rests on
the picnic chair, the basket on the ground, and Maya carries the final lantern.

LakeFirstFive and LakePicnic drive the two checkpoint transitions. The next
scene begins one second after each recording ends, unless the user continues
or exits manually. LakeMeet plays at Lily's reunion before Kids Remix.

Animations respect reduced-motion preferences. Recordings were copied locally;
no transcription service was used.
