Children and parental choices

SmartClass is designed mainly for children aged 3–6. Parent sign-in and purchases are adult activities. Children do not need a Google account to play the free learning experiences. Parents may use a child’s nickname and may leave optional family names blank.

Personal information about a child requires the authority or prior consent of a parent, guardian or other competent person where applicable. A parent gate is not, by itself, consent to every use of children’s data. Contact our privacy contact if information was entered without appropriate authority, to withdraw consent, or to request access, correction or deletion.

The current learning experience has no third-party advertising, advertising profiles, public child accounts, child-to-child messaging or sale of children’s information. We do not ask children for their address, school, identity number, precise location, contacts, photograph or voice recording. Please do not enter identifying information in a drawing or send it in support requests unless it is needed to resolve the request.

Drawing checks and automated feedback

The tracing feature first compares strokes on your device. For some drawings it then sends a rendered image of the drawn strokes and the target numeral to our server for an OpenAI vision check. It does not deliberately attach your parent email, child nickname or learning report to that request. The image may nevertheless contain anything drawn on the canvas, so avoid writing personal information there.

Our drawing endpoint does not save submitted drawings in an application database and uses the API’s store: false setting. This setting is not a guarantee of zero retention by the provider. OpenAI’s standard API abuse-monitoring retention may apply, normally up to 30 days, with exceptions described in its data controls documentation. Its standard API terms do not use API content to train models by default. We do not claim that our project has an approved Zero Data Retention arrangement without verifying it.

The result is limited to learning feedback such as a match, retry or uncertainty. It is not used for a decision about admission, eligibility, a child’s health or legal rights. Local checks can also provide feedback when the remote service is unavailable. Parents can avoid transmitting a drawing by not submitting a tracing check and can continue using other activities.

Service providers may process information outside South Africa, including in the United States. Provider terms and safeguards must be maintained; we do not promise that all processing takes place in South Africa.

Cookies, storage and retention

  • Sign-in: the current parent session cookie expires after seven days; the sign-in security challenge expires after ten minutes. Signing out clears the active browser’s session cookies. A signed session is not a permanent server-side account record.
  • Local learning report: the app filters activity older than 90 days when it loads and also limits stored event counts. If you do not reopen it, old browser storage may remain until you clear it. Optional profile names and preferences remain until changed or cleared.
  • Game and media storage: progress, preview usage, preferences and cached narration can remain until you clear site data or your browser removes it. Clearing a report alone does not clear every game’s storage.
  • Drawing checks: we do not keep a drawing archive in the tracing endpoint. Provider retention is described above. A short-lived hashed network identifier is used for request-rate limiting.
  • Operational logs: hosting/security logs follow the relevant provider’s configured retention and any lawful incident-preservation need. They are not an indefinite learning-history archive.
  • Support and future paid transactions: records are kept only as long as needed to resolve the issue, administer the transaction and meet applicable accounting, tax, fraud-prevention or legal duties. A deletion response will identify any records we must retain and the applicable reason or period.

The current website uses functional sign-in cookies, browser storage and media caches. It does not deploy an advertising cookie banner as a substitute for consent. If optional analytics or marketing technologies are added, they must be disclosed and any required consent obtained before they operate.

Security

We use HTTPS for transmission, server-side credential checks, protected sign-in cookies and restricted server-side API credentials. Drawing requests have size and rate limits. Access to administrative systems should be limited to authorised staff. No system is risk-free; if a security compromise requires notification under applicable law, we will notify the relevant regulator and affected people as required.

Never send passwords, card security codes or banking one-time passwords to our support team. Use your browser and device’s security features on shared devices, and sign out when finished.

Your rights and requests

You can ask to access, correct or delete personal information, withdraw consent where applicable, object to processing where the law permits, and complain about our handling of information. A parent or guardian can make a request about a child for whom they have authority. We may ask for proportionate verification to avoid giving someone else access; we do not routinely ask you to email identity documents.

Use support@smartclassedu.com, addressed to Neo Mashigo. Tell us the account email and the nature of your request, using the minimum information needed. If we need more information or must retain particular records by law, we will explain why. Requests are handled within applicable legal time limits.

See Account and data requests for current browser-data controls and deletion-request instructions. Deleting data and cancelling a paid subscription are separate actions; see cancellation instructions as well.

You can complain to South Africa’s Information Regulator, or another competent authority where applicable. You do not need our permission to do so.
