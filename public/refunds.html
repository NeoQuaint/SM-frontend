The simple version

First direct website payment: request your money back within 7 calendar days.
Monthly renewal: cancel before the next renewal to stop future charges.
Apple or Google billing: use the store’s subscription and refund tools.
Current website: selecting a package is a free preview. It does not take payment, create a paid subscription or authorise recurring charges. The purchase terms below apply only when you complete an actual, clearly identified paid checkout.

This policy forms part of the Terms and Conditions. It covers SmartClass digital learning access. There are no physical goods to return.

7-day first-payment guarantee

We will fully refund your first direct website subscription payment if you request it within seven calendar days after payment. You do not need to give a reason, and using the activities does not disqualify you. We will cancel the associated renewal; refunded access may end when the refund is confirmed.

This voluntary guarantee applies once to the first subscription payment for a customer. Renewals, repeat sign-ups and upgrades do not restart it. This does not limit refunds for incorrect billing, non-delivery, defective service or any right given by law.

Cancel future website payments

Email support@smartclassedu.com from the purchasing email address. Include the package and, if available, the transaction reference. Send your request before the next renewal. We will confirm it and stop future renewal charges without a cancellation fee.

A request received before renewal is timely even if our team processes it afterwards. Any renewal taken after that timely request will be refunded. Without a refund, you can continue using the paid package until the current paid period ends. Simply not using SmartClass does not cancel a subscription.

Incorrect charges and service problems

Contact us if you were charged twice, charged the wrong amount, charged after a timely cancellation, did not authorise a payment or did not receive purchased access. We will investigate the payment and access records and correct the issue. Do not send complete card details or banking credentials.

Outside the first-payment guarantee, cancellation alone normally does not create a refund for an already-used billing period. However, we will provide refunds, repairs to access, price reductions or other remedies where required by law or justified by a failure to deliver the purchased service. If we discontinue a paid service without an equivalent replacement, we will refund the unused part of the paid period.

Requesting and receiving a refund

  1. Email support@smartclassedu.com with your purchasing email, payment date, amount and transaction reference if available.
  2. Say whether you are using the first-payment guarantee or reporting a billing/service issue. No reason is required for the guarantee.
  3. We will verify the transaction, confirm the outcome and explain any information needed to complete it.

Approved refunds go back through the original payment route where supported. If that is not possible, we will agree a verified alternative with you; we will not ask for passwords or one-time passwords. We do not deduct an administration fee from a refund under the first-payment guarantee or a refund for our billing error.

We aim to review requests within five business days and initiate approved refunds within ten business days after verification, subject to any shorter period required by law. The time until funds appear depends on the payment method and bank. These service targets do not extend a statutory deadline. We will provide an update if an investigation takes longer.

Subscriptions billed by an app store

If Apple billed you, manage cancellation at Apple subscriptions and request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. If Google Play billed you, use Google Play subscriptions and its refund process. The store determines eligibility under its rules and applicable law.

Our voluntary first-payment website guarantee applies to direct website transactions, not store-billed purchases. We will still help investigate SmartClass access problems. Removing an app or deleting a SmartClass profile does not cancel store billing.

Your statutory rights remain

Nothing in this policy excludes or reduces mandatory rights under the South African Consumer Protection Act, Electronic Communications and Transactions Act or any other applicable consumer law. Where a statutory cooling-off or cancellation right applies, we will honour it and its refund deadlines. We do not automatically treat opening a lesson as a waiver of every cooling-off right.

You may raise a dispute with a competent consumer body, regulator, payment provider or court. Contacting us first can help resolve an issue, but does not remove your legal rights.
