The learning service

SmartClass provides digital early-learning activities intended mainly for children aged 3–6, with adult supervision. Content includes counting, colours, shapes, songs, object and action vocabulary, finding games, alphabets, spelling and illustrated stories. Parent reports describe recorded activity; they are not clinical assessments, school qualifications or guarantees of learning outcomes.

Access is delivered online. There is no physical shipment or delivery charge. You need a compatible browser or supported app, a device with sound where appropriate, and an internet connection. Your network provider’s data charges apply. Adult support and regular screen breaks are encouraged.
