Ask about or delete your information

Parents and guardians can request deletion of their SmartClass information or a child’s information they are authorised to manage. Email support@smartclassedu.com from the parent email address used for sign-in. State whether you want all associated information deleted or only a particular item. Do not include the child’s full name or identity documents unless we specifically explain why verification is necessary.

We will verify authority, explain which information we hold, and confirm deletion or identify any lawful retention requirement. Signing out alone is not a request to erase retained support or payment records. You do not need to buy a subscription to make a privacy request.

Current website account model: Google sign-in creates a seven-day browser session, not a separate permanent SmartClass account record in our authentication database. Local game data must be cleared on each device and each site address you used. The request above also covers information we may hold through support or service providers; we will explain any limitations or required retention.

Clear learning information on your device

  1. To clear the learning dashboard only, open Grown-ups and choose Clear this learner’s report. This removes that report and its family names on the current browser. Other game data remains.
  2. To remove all local SmartClass data, use your browser’s site settings to clear cookies, local/site storage and cached data for smartclasss.com and www.smartclasss.com, if both appear. This removes local progress, preferences and sign-in on that browser.
  3. Repeat on any other browser or device used. Export a report first if you want your own copy; clearing local data cannot be undone.

These browser actions do not erase information held by payment providers or support systems, revoke Google permissions, or cancel subscriptions. Use the request process for server/provider information. You can separately review SmartClass access in your Google account connections; this does not delete your Google account.

What may remain and why

Information that must be kept for accounting, legal claims, fraud prevention or another lawful obligation may be retained for the required period, with restricted use. We will identify the relevant category and reason in our response. Provider security logs and backup cycles may also affect when all copies expire; we will not represent signing out as instant deletion from every provider.

See the Privacy Policy’s retention section for the current session, browser and drawing-check details. Cancelling a subscription is a separate step: follow the Refunds and Cancellations Policy.
